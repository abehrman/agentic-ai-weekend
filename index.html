The practical questions

Before you join.

Is this for complete beginners?

Yes. The course begins with an ordinary AI task and builds one capability at a time. You need everyday laptop comfort, not coding experience.

What exactly will I build?

You will create a Personal Agent Map, configure a local assistant, add approved memory, connect it to Slack, complete one practical mission, and leave with verification and permissions methods.

Can it help with financial or medical choices?

It can help organize research, compare scenarios, surface sources, and prepare better questions. It does not replace a qualified financial, legal, or medical professional, diagnose conditions, or execute portfolio decisions.

Where is the course held?

In person in Tenafly, New Jersey. The exact venue and arrival details are shared with enrolled learners.

What software do I need?

Bring the supported laptop described above with permission to install software. Setup for Hermes Desktop, Hindsight, and Slack is guided during preflight and class.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Learners who complete the course receive a named Certificate of Completion issued by AgentX AI Course with the program dates, total program length, and a unique credential ID. It does not confer academic credit and is not a professional certification.

How does enrollment work?

Review the cohort details, complete secure payment through Stripe, and receive the onboarding and preflight steps by email.
