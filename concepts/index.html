Prototype 01 / Spatial

Concrete, inspectable, stoppable. A teaching workshop rather than a software diagram.

The physical model

The Clearance Bench

Bring the work. Clamp the brief. Inspect the evidence. Nothing leaves the bench until you operate the release.

  1. Clamp the brief. The learner sets goal, context, constraints, and output.
  2. Read the route. The agent proposes a plan before work begins.
  3. Seat approved tools. Unapproved sockets remain physically empty.
  4. Inspect and correct. Unsupported claims return; memory changes require a decision.
  5. Review and release. The stop remains down until the learner approves.

No autoplay. One deliberate activation advances one clearance. The final activation is labeled Review and release.

Prototype 03 / Participatory

A compressed rehearsal of outcome, evidence, authority, return, and approval.

Direction leaves visible marks

The Working Score

Choose what should return. Lay down proof. Set the edge of authority. Send one unsupported claim back. Approve only the corrected draft.

  1. Choose an output. One-page plan or task checklist; neither is presented as the right answer.
  2. Lay down proof. Support dates and costs, and flag what cannot be supported.
  3. Set the stop line. Draft only; do not send or publish; return for review.
  4. Return the unsupported claim. “Permit due June 1” has no support and returns as an honest open question.
  5. Approve the corrected draft. Approval is for the visitor’s use; nothing is sent or published.

This is a working score, not onboarding: no app frame, settings, progress meter, quiz, chat, score, or account state.
