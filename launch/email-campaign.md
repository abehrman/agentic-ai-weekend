**Subject:** Chatbot, agent, agentic system: the useful difference  
**Preheader:** Learn the language, then build the system.

You do not need to become a programmer to understand agentic AI.

You do need a clear mental model.

In the October AgentX course, we build that model one layer at a time:

1. An **LLM** understands and generates language from your instructions and context.
2. An **agent** uses that LLM in a loop: plan, act, check, and adjust.
