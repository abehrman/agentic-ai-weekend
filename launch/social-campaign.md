## LinkedIn — follow-up 1: teach the distinction

A chatbot answers.

An agent works toward a goal.

That difference becomes clear when you see the full loop:

**Plan → act → check → adjust.**

The LLM is the language engine. An agent adds a goal and a loop. An agentic system adds approved tools, memory, evidence, permissions, and human approval.

The human approval step matters. You decide what the system may access, what it may remember, and whether a result is ready to use.

This is one of the core models in the October AgentX AI Course. Learners do not stop at the vocabulary. They map the parts, configure a personal assistant, test it on real work, and learn how to correct it.

No coding experience is required.
