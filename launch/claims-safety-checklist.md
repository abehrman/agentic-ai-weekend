# AgentX claims and safety checklist

Use this before publishing, sending, or approving any marketing asset.

- Enrollment is capped at 20 participants.

## Approved positioning

- “Build your second-brain” describes a personal assistant organized around context and memory the learner approves.
- “Work with superhuman reach” describes extending human capacity for research, planning, organization, and creation. It does not mean perfect, autonomous, or error-free work.
- The system can help organize financial or portfolio research, compare scenarios, and prepare questions. It does not provide or execute investment, tax, or legal advice.
- The system can help research doctors, gather sources, and prepare questions. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a medical professional.
- The learner remains responsible for checking evidence and approving important outputs.

## Do not publish

- Do not call AgentX a university, college, accredited institution, academic program, or university partner.
- Do not state or imply transferable credit, a professional license, guaranteed employment, guaranteed income, or guaranteed return on investment.
- Do not promise correct, unbiased, private, secure, autonomous, or error-free AI output.
- Do not state that AI replaces a doctor, lawyer, accountant, financial advisor, or the learner’s judgment.
- Do not use fabricated testimonials, learner counts, outcomes, ratings, partnerships, press mentions, or employer endorsements.
- Do not write “selling fast,” “almost full,” “only X seats left,” or similar scarcity language without a current paid-enrollment count.
- Do not call a price deadline “extended,” “one time only,” or “last chance” unless the published terms and actual offer support that statement.
- Do not imply that the assistant automatically learns everything, sees private accounts, or acts without explicit tools and permission.
