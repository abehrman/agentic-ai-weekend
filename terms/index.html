Enrollment terms.

Effective August 26, 2026

Course

Nine in-person sessions in Tenafly, New Jersey, October 5–27, 2026, from 6:30–9:00 PM. The exact venue is sent to enrolled participants.

Cohort size

Enrollment is limited to 20 participants.

Tuition

The amount shown at Stripe checkout is the tuition due for the applicable enrollment window. Personal travel or equipment costs are separate.

Cancellation, refund, and transfer

Cancel in writing through September 28, 2026 for a full tuition refund. After September 28, tuition is non-refundable. You may transfer the enrollment to another eligible participant through October 2, 2026 by contacting AgentX AI Course. Missed sessions do not create a partial refund.

If AgentX AI Course cancels the course

If AgentX AI Course cancels the cohort, AgentX AI Course will refund paid tuition in full.

Participation and technology

Learners are responsible for bringing a supported laptop and having permission to install software. Supported computers are a Mac with an M1, M2, M3, M4, or newer M-series chip, or a Windows 10/11 laptop. Mac users can open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac to find the Chip label. Macs showing an Intel processor are not supported for this cohort.

Responsible use

The course teaches research, planning, organization, and controlled agent workflows. It does not provide medical, legal, tax, or investment advice. Participants must keep sensitive personal or confidential information out of shared exercises.

Independent program

The Agentic AI Intensive is the October cohort from AgentX AI Course. AgentX AI Course is independent and is not university affiliated, accredited, or offered for transferable academic credit.
