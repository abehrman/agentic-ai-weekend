Stripe collects the name, email, and payment details needed to process enrollment. AgentX AI Course receives the enrollment and payment record. Enrollment information is used to confirm payment, send course logistics, run preflight, support participation, keep required business records, prevent fraud, and respond to course questions.

Payment data

Card details are entered on Stripe-hosted checkout and are handled under Stripe’s privacy and security practices. This website does not receive or store full card details.

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate enrollment and the course, or when required by law. AgentX AI Course does not sell enrollment information. Records are retained only as long as needed for course operations, business records, disputes, and legal obligations.
